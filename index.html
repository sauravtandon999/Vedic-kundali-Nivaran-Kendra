Numerology & Lo Shu Grid

Numerology uses numbers derived from a person's date of birth and, in some systems, their name. The Lo Shu Grid is a traditional 3×3 number arrangement used to discuss digit patterns.

Birth Number

Traditionally calculated from the day of birth and reduced to a single digit.

Life Path Number

Commonly calculated by adding the digits of the complete birth date.

Name Numerology

Some numerology systems assign numerical values to letters and interpret the resulting total.

Frequently Asked Questions

What details are needed for Kundali analysis?

Date of birth, exact birth time and birth place are normally requested for a personalised birth chart. Missing or inaccurate details can affect the interpretation.

Is the Lo Shu Grid the same as a Vedic Kundali?

No. The Lo Shu Grid is a numerology method based on date digits. A Vedic birth chart uses birth details and planetary calculations.

Can I get a gemstone suggestion for free?

You can explore the website's numerology calculator and gemstone guide. Its output is educational and should not be treated as a guaranteed personalised prescription.

Does wearing a gemstone guarantee success?

No. Such outcomes are not scientifically established. Avoid making medical, legal or financial decisions based solely on astrology.
